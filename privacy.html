Privacy Policy

Rclone Google Drive Sync is a personal-use application that uses the Google Drive API to synchronize files between my personal computer and my Google Drive.

Data Access

The application accesses Google Drive files and metadata for the purpose of file synchronization.

Data Storage

Files are stored locally on my computer and in my Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are stored locally by Rclone.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share Google user data with advertisers or other third parties.

Data Retention

Files remain stored locally or in Google Drive until deleted by the account owner or through synchronization. Authorization can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

This application is maintained exclusively for personal use.
